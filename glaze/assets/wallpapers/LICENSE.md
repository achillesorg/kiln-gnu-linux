# Wallpaper licence

All wallpapers in this directory (the v2 sets `kiln`, `draft`, `margin`,
`night-paper`, `classic-teal`, and the legacy v1 sets `paper-hills` and
`terracotta-waves`) are **original artwork** made for Glaze / Kiln GNU/Linux.
They are generated procedurally by `scripts/gen-wallpapers.py` from shapes
and colours defined in that script; the paper grain is generated noise, not a
scan. No third-party images, fonts, icon packs or illustrations are used, and
nothing is derived from Anthropic, Microsoft or any other party's artwork.
